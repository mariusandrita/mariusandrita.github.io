# Quick Chicken Soup with Vegetables 🍲
*Быстрый куриный супчик с овощами*

![The finished soup in a bowl, pot behind](images/kurinyj-supchik/finished.jpg)
*Photo: cover of the [reel by Shabnami Rustam](https://www.facebook.com/share/r/18Y59L8VxM/)*

> **Source:** the recipe comes from the reel above (2.4 M views). The reel gives ingredients and method but **no amounts for water, pasta or salt**; I filled those from Russian cooking sites and marked them *(typical)*.

## The story
- **Origins:** Chicken soup is ancient as a remedy: Hippocrates and Avicenna are said to have recommended it for weak patients, and the physician Maimonides prescribed chicken broth in the Middle Ages ([Zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/stati/6510903-den-kurinogo-supalapshi-pravda-li-chto-on-lechit-i-kak-sdelat-blyudo-poleznym.html), search summary). In Slavic countries it became an everyday dish, often with homemade egg noodles.
- **How it evolved:** The classic version simmers a hen for 1.5 hours and fries the onion and carrot first. This reel's version skips both: everything cooks together, then the **vegetables are blended** into the broth for body, and the soup is done in under an hour. I found nothing specific on the history of the dish in Rus itself.
- **Fun fact:** Chicken broth is famous as a cold remedy, thanks to the amino acid cysteine ([Zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/stati/6510903-den-kurinogo-supalapshi-pravda-li-chto-on-lechit-i-kak-sdelat-blyudo-poleznym.html)). Hot soup does help with warmth and fluids; "cures a cold" is the popular claim, not proven.

## Grandma's tips 👵
*From Russian sites that describe the old-fashioned (babushka) method:*
- **Start in cold water and bring it to a boil slowly, then skim the foam completely.** That is the key to a clear broth. *Confirmed by several sources* ([UNIAN](https://www.unian.net/lite/advice/kak-cdelat-kurinyy-bulon-vkusnee-dostatochno-ispolzovat-eti-3-ingredienta-12750144.html), [E1](https://www.e1.ru/text/food/2026/03/29/76333550/)).
- **Keep the onion in its husk** (peel only the top layer): the husk gives a golden colour and the onion boosts the chicken flavour. *Tradition* (UNIAN). In this recipe the onion is blended, so the colour comes from the carrots and tomato.
- **Add dill at the very end, in moderation.** *Tradition* (UNIAN).
- **Let the soup rest, covered, off the heat.** E1 says 10–15 minutes, Lady.mail.ru says 20–30. *Confirmed* ([E1](https://www.e1.ru/text/food/2026/03/29/76333550/), [Lady.mail.ru](https://lady.mail.ru/recipe/24757-sup-na-kurinom-bulone-s-vermishelju/)).
- **Cook the pasta separately if you will keep leftovers,** so it doesn't turn soggy overnight. *Grandma tip* (E1).
- **For a richer broth use a soup hen and simmer at least 1.5 hours.** *Tradition,* for the slow version (UNIAN).

**Serves** 5–6 · **Prep** 15 min · **Cook** 35 min · **Total** about 55 min · **Easy**

> **Before you start:** take out a 3 L pot and an immersion blender, and wash the dill or parsley.

## Mise en place
- [ ] Peel 2 potatoes, 2 carrots, 1 onion and 1 garlic clove
- [ ] Cut the potatoes and carrots into chunks, the onion into quarters, and seed and quarter the pepper and tomato
- [ ] Measure 2.5 L cold water
- [ ] Chop the dill or parsley and squeeze half a lemon
- [ ] Have the immersion blender and a plate for the chicken ready

## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 chicken breasts
- [ ] 2 potatoes
- [ ] 2 carrots
- [ ] 1 onion
- [ ] 1 bell pepper
- [ ] 1 tomato
- [ ] 1 clove garlic
- [ ] 2.5 L cold water *(typical, for a 3 L pot)*
- [ ] 1 tsp salt to start, more to taste *(typical)*
- [ ] 80 g fine pasta or vermicelli, about 2 small handfuls *(typical; "a little" in the reel)*
- [ ] A handful of fresh dill or parsley
- [ ] Juice of ½ lemon

## Steps
1. [ ] Put **2 chicken breasts**, **2 potatoes**, **2 carrots**, **1 onion**, **1 bell pepper**, **1 tomato** and **1 garlic clove** into the pot. Pour in **2.5 L cold water** and add **1 tsp salt**.
2. [ ] Bring to the boil over medium-high heat, **skim all the foam**, then lower to a gentle simmer.
   ⏱ **25 min** — *chicken white right through, potatoes and carrots soft.* *(typical; the reel says only "until done")*
3. [ ] Lift the chicken out onto a plate and let it cool slightly, then shred it into pieces.
4. [ ] Take the pot off the heat and blend the **vegetables** with an immersion blender until smooth. *Keep the chicken out so the blender doesn't catch it.*
5. [ ] Return the shredded chicken to the soup. Bring to a simmer and add **80 g pasta**. Cook it **1–2 minutes less than the packet says**.
   ⏱ **5 min** — *pasta just tender.*
6. [ ] Turn off the heat. Stir in **a handful of chopped dill or parsley** and the **juice of ½ lemon**. Taste and add salt if needed. Cover and rest.
   ⏱ **10 min** — *flavours settle; the pasta finishes softening.*

## Timer cheat-sheet
| Step | Time | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| Simmer | 25 min | Chicken white inside, vegetables soft |
| Pasta | 5 min | Just tender |
| Rest | 10 min | Covered, off the heat |

## If it goes wrong
- **Cloudy soup:** it boiled too hard or the foam wasn't skimmed. Simmer gently and skim.
- **Bland:** add salt in small pinches and a bit more lemon.
- **Too thick:** add hot water a splash at a time.
- **Dry chicken:** don't cook the breasts much beyond 25 minutes; shred them straight away.
- **Soggy pasta the next day:** cook pasta separately for leftovers.

## Serve, store, reheat
Serve hot with dark bread, extra dill and a lemon wedge. Keeps 2–3 days in the fridge; reheat gently and add water if it has thickened. The broth alone freezes well.

## Variants
- **Clear broth version:** keep the onion in its husk, fry the grated carrot briefly in oil, and don't blend.
- **Lemon-free:** the lemon is the reel's finish; skip it if you prefer a plain broth.
