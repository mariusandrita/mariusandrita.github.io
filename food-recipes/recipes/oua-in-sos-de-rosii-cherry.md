# Eggs in tomato sauce, from a jar of cherry tomatoes 🇷🇴
*Ouă în sos de roșii: onion and pepper, your grandma's jarred cherry tomatoes and their juice, and eggs cooked right in the pan*

![Eggs cooked in a red tomato sauce in a pan, with feta and parsley](images/oua-in-sos-de-rosii-cherry/finished.jpg)
*Photo: [Gustos](https://www.gustos.ro/retete-culinare/shakshuka-reteta-de-oua-in-sos-de-rosii-la-tigaie.html)*

## The story
- **Origins:** Eggs cooked in a tomato sauce are a classic of North Africa. The Romanian press calls the dish **shakshuka**, a Berber word for "a mix", and places its birth in the Tunisia–Libya region; it later travelled across the Middle East ([Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stil-de-viata/bucatarie/una-dintre-cele-mai-sanatoase-mancaruri-din-lume-2476666.html)). Where exactly it began is disputed.
- **How it evolved:** On Romanian recipe sites it appears under plain names: *ouă în sos de roșii*, *ochiuri în sos de roșii*, *mâncare de roșii cu ouă* ([Bucătaras](https://www.bucataras.ro/retete/oua-in-sos-de-rosii-9480.html), [BZI](https://www.bzi.ro/mancare-de-rosii-cu-oua-o-vei-manca-in-fiecare-dimineata-5038518)). One Bucătaras cook uses just a little **tomato juice** with a pepper and a single egg, plus sweet corn ([Ou ochi în sos de roșii](https://www.bucataras.ro/retete/ou-ochi-in-sos-de-rosii-65021.html)), which is much like what you have in the jar. *I found no source that says when the dish reached Romanian kitchens, so I don't give a date.*
- **Fun fact:** a reader of the Bucătaras recipe commented that the base dish is shakshuka and that it is Jewish; the sites I read mostly say it came to Israel with Jews from the Maghreb.

## Cooks' tips 👵
*No named grandmother found. Your own grandma's jar is the "grandma" here. The tips below come from Romanian recipe sites.*
- **Soften the vegetables covered, on low heat; don't fry them hard** (Bucătaras, shakshuka). **[confirmed by 2 sources]**
- **Crush the tomatoes with a fork** right in the pan, juice and all (Bucătaras, Ouă în sos de roșii). **[tradition]**
- **For soft yolks, lift the lid now and then.** Take the pan off the heat as soon as the whites are set and leave the lid on for 1 more minute (Bucătaras, shakshuka; Gustos adjusts the time to how firm you want the yolk). **[confirmed by 2 sources]**
- **Use very fresh eggs** so the yolk stays whole (BZI). **[tradition]**
- **If the sauce tastes sharp, add a pinch of sugar or a little honey** (BZI). **[tradition]**
- **Add cheese right after cracking the eggs,** so it melts a little (BZI), or crumble feta on top when done (Gustos). **[tradition]**
- **Serve it hot in the pan with fresh or toasted bread** to dip (Gustos, Bucătaras, BZI). **[confirmed by 3 sources]**

**Serves** 2 · **Prep** 10 min · **Cook** 25 min · **Total** about 35 min · **Difficulty** easy

> **Before you start:** open the jar and **check it**: a bulging lid, a bad smell, bubbles or mould means *throw it away*. Then **taste a spoon of the juice**: if it is salty, don't salt the sauce until the end. You need a wide **frying pan with a lid**.

## Mise en place
- [ ] Open the jar; lift out **about 450 g of tomatoes** (roughly ¾ of a 700 ml jar) and keep the juice in the jar
- [ ] Chop **1 onion** small; cut **1 red bell pepper** into thin strips; chop **3 garlic cloves**
- [ ] Chop a small handful of **parsley**
- [ ] Put the **4 eggs** next to the stove (room temperature is easier on the yolk)
- [ ] Bread ready to slice or toast

## Ingredients
- [ ] **450 g cherry tomatoes** from the jar *(about ¾ of a 700 ml jar; whole, half-skinned)*
- [ ] **about 100 ml of the jar's tomato juice** (keep the rest of the juice for soup or sauce)
- [ ] 4 eggs, very fresh
- [ ] 1 onion
- [ ] 1 red bell pepper (about 200 g)
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves
- [ ] 2 tbsp oil (sunflower or olive)
- [ ] 1 tsp sweet paprika
- [ ] ½ tsp ground cumin (*chimen*), optional
- [ ] Salt and black pepper (add salt only after tasting the juice)
- [ ] 1 pinch sugar, if the sauce is sharp
- [ ] Parsley, a small handful
- [ ] Optional: 50 g feta, crumbled; a few slices of hot pepper
- [ ] **To serve:** fresh bread, or toasted bread

## Steps
1. [ ] **Soften the vegetables:** heat **2 tbsp oil** in a wide pan over **medium-low** heat. Add the **chopped onion** and the **pepper strips**, cover, and cook, stirring now and then.
   ⏱ **Timer: 6 min** — *soft and a little golden, not browned.*
2. [ ] **Spices:** add **3 chopped garlic cloves**, **1 tsp paprika** and **½ tsp cumin**. Stir.
   ⏱ **Timer: 1–2 min** — *you can smell the garlic; don't let the paprika burn.*
3. [ ] **Tomatoes:** add the **450 g jar tomatoes** and **about 100 ml of their juice**. Crush them in the pan with a fork or the back of a spoon; the half-loose skins can stay, just chop them in with the spatula. Add **a little black pepper**.
4. [ ] **Simmer** on **low** until the sauce is thick and the spoon leaves a trail.
   ⏱ **Timer: 10–15 min** — *sauce thick, not watery. If it gets too thick, add a splash of jar juice.*
5. [ ] **Taste:** add **salt** if needed (the jar may already be salty) and **a pinch of sugar** if the sauce is sharp.
6. [ ] **Make wells:** with the back of a spoon make **4 hollows** in the sauce. Crack **1 egg** into each, one by one. *Crack each egg into a cup first if you worry about shells or a broken yolk.* Season the eggs with a little salt and pepper. If you use cheese, add the **crumbled feta** now.
7. [ ] **Cook the eggs:** put the lid on, **low** heat. Lift the lid at **4 minutes** to check.
   ⏱ **Timer: 4–5 min** — *whites set and milky-white, yolks still soft and glossy.* Take the pan off the heat and leave the lid on **1 minute**.
8. [ ] **Serve:** sprinkle **parsley**, bring the pan to the table, and eat with **bread** for dipping.

## Timer cheat-sheet
| Step | Time | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Onion and pepper | 6 min | Soft, lightly golden |
| 2 Garlic and spices | 1–2 min | Fragrant, not burnt |
| 4 Simmer | 10–15 min | Thick sauce |
| 7 Eggs | 4–5 min | Whites set, yolks soft |

## If it goes wrong
- **Sauce too watery:** simmer a few minutes more before the eggs. A wet sauce keeps the whites from setting.
- **Whites still clear:** put the lid back for 1 more minute, on low heat.
- **Yolks hard:** the eggs stayed too long. Check at 4 min and use the heat-off minute next time.
- **Too salty:** the jar juice was salty. Add a pinch of sugar and a spoon of water, and don't add more salt.
- **Too sharp or sour:** a pinch of sugar or a little honey.
- **Tomato skins in the sauce:** chop them with the spatula, or push the sauce through a coarse sieve before the eggs go in.

## Serve, store, reheat
Eat straight from the pan, hot, with bread. This dish is best at once; if there are leftovers, refrigerate them within 2 hours and eat within 1 day. A runny yolk means the egg is not fully cooked, so children, elderly people, pregnant women and anyone ill should cook the eggs until the yolks are firm (add a minute or two). The leftover jar juice keeps in the fridge in a closed jar for a few days.

**How I reconciled them:** tomatoes per 4 eggs: 1 can (about 400 g), 400 g, 400 g fresh, 700 g, 750 g fresh. The canned and in-juice figures cluster at 400 g; I use **450 g** plus **100 ml** of juice, because the jar's tomatoes are whole and drain well. Sauce simmer: 15 min, 10–15 min, "until the juice reduces" → **10–15 min**. Eggs covered: 4 min, 5 min, 5–7 min, "until the whites set" → **4–5 min, check at 4**. Oil: 2, 2, 2–3, 3–4 tbsp → **2 tbsp**. Garlic: 2–4 cloves → **3**. Paprika 1 tsp (Gustos, Jamila) → **1 tsp**; cumin ½–1 tsp → **½ tsp, optional**. Onion and pepper: 1 each in four sources → **1 each**. I did not use the Sezamo pages (English translations, not native-language sources). I read Bucătaras's *ouă jumări cu ceapă, ardei și roșii* only as a variant; Laura Laurențiu's *menemen* I saw only in search results and read just its ingredient list.

## Regional variants & substitutions
- **Ouă jumări / menemen** (stirred, creamy): beat the eggs and stir them into the vegetables until set instead of leaving them whole ([Bucătaras, ouă jumări cu ceapă, ardei și roșii](https://www.bucataras.ro/retete/oua-jumari-cu-ceapa-ardei-si-rosii-95698.html)).
- **With potatoes** ([Bucătaras, Tigaie cu roșii, cartofi și ouă](https://www.bucataras.ro/retete/tigaie-cu-rosii-cartofi-si-oua-93232.html)): roast 3 diced potatoes at 200 °C for 20–25 min, add them to the sauce before the eggs; oregano and basil.
- **One egg, quick** (Bucătaras, Ou ochi): pepper, about 100 ml tomato juice and sweet corn, covered 10 min.
- **Feta on top** (Gustos) or Greek yogurt (Laura Laurențiu).
- **Hotter:** hot pepper, chili flakes (Gustos) or harissa (Laura Laurențiu).

## Confidence note
The Romanian sources agree on the method: soften onion and pepper, simmer tomatoes, crack eggs in, cover. The quantities and times are medians, and the jar adaptation (450 g plus 100 ml of juice, crushing whole half-skinned cherry tomatoes) is my own: none of the pages use jarred cherry tomatoes. I did not cook it.
