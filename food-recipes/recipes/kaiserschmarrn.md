# Kaiserschmarrn 🇦🇹
*Der Kaiserschmarrn: fluffy shredded pancake with raisins and plum compote*

![Kaiserschmarrn dusted with icing sugar on a plate](images/kaiserschmarrn/finished.jpg)
*Photo: [Gute Küche](https://www.gutekueche.at/oesterreichischer-kaiserschmarrn-rezept-4326)*

## The story
- **Origins:** The word *Schmarren* first appears in print in 1563, in a wedding sermon by Johannes Mathesius ("feister Schmarren"). The batter dish shows up as *Mehlschmarrn* in cookbooks by F. G. Zenker (1824) and Franz Zelena (1831). The oldest known mention of **"Kaiserschmarrn"** is on a menu of the inn "Zum Sperl" around 1835 ([Wien Geschichte Wiki](https://www.geschichtewiki.wien.gv.at/Schmarren)).
- **How it evolved:** The famous story says a court cook made a light pancake for Empress Elisabeth ("Sisi"), it came out too thick and tore, so he ripped it into pieces and caramelised it with sugar. Franz Joseph is said to have answered: *"Na, dann gebt mir halt diesen Schmarrn, den der Koch da für die Kaiserin gemacht hat."* ([Gute Küche](https://www.gutekueche.at/des-kaisers-schmarrn-artikel-4758)). But the Wien Geschichte Wiki says it does not stand up to serious research: in 1835 Franz Joseph was five years old and Sisi was not yet born. More likely, "Kaiser-" just means *top quality*, as in *Kaisersemmel*.
- **Fun fact:** *Schmarren* comes from *Schmer* (fat) and *schmieren* (to grease). In everyday Austrian speech it also means "nonsense", so "So ein Schmarrn!" is a mild way to say that is rubbish.

## Austrian cooks' tips 👵
*I found no named grandmother for this dish, so these are tips from Austrian recipe sites and cooks.*
- **Check the Eischnee:** a raw or cooked egg should sink halfway into the beaten whites. **Don't over-beat** them, or they lose their stability. *Confirmed* ([Servus](https://www.servus.com/a/gk/kaiserschmarrn-goldene-regeln-rezepte)).
- **Fold the Eischnee in only briefly** ("nur mehr wenig durchrühren") so it stays airy. *Confirmed* ([Gute Küche](https://www.gutekueche.at/oesterreichischer-kaiserschmarrn-rezept-4326)).
- **Use moderate heat and take your time:** at high heat the egg proteins set before the steam can lift the batter. *Confirmed* (Servus).
- **Soak the raisins in rum (or rum water)** and put them into the batter at the end. A thick batter lets them sink slowly without burning on the bottom (chef Michael Vesely, Servus). *Tradition.*
- **Batter too thick? Add milk. Too thin? Add flour.** ([Gute Küche](https://www.gutekueche.at/oesterreichischer-kaiserschmarrn-rezept-4326)). *Confirmed.*
- **No flipping needed:** start it on the stove, then finish it under the oven grill on the lower rack ([Servus](https://www.servus.com/r/kaiserschmarrn-mit-zwetschkenroester)), or bake it in the oven at 180 °C for 8–10 minutes ([Essen & Trinken](https://www.essen-und-trinken.de/rezepte/kaiserschmarrn-rezept-13360656.html)). *Variants.*
- **Caramelise at the end:** sugar and butter go in after tearing, and you toss the pieces. *Confirmed* (Servus, Gute Küche).
- **To drink:** coffee, or a Gewürztraminer Auslese with some residual sugar (Servus).

**Serves** 3–4 · **Prep** 20 min · **Cook** 15 min · **Easy to medium**

> **Before you start:** soak the raisins at least 30 minutes ahead and make the plum compote first (it needs to cool slightly). You need a large non-stick or cast-iron pan (about 28 cm) and two forks.

## Mise en place
- [ ] Soak **40 g raisins** in **3 tbsp rum** (or water)
- [ ] Separate **4 eggs** (egg whites in a very clean, grease-free bowl)
- [ ] Weigh **150 g flour**, **30 g sugar** + **20 g sugar** for the end, **40 g butter**
- [ ] Halve and stone **500 g plums** for the compote
- [ ] Two forks and a spatula

## Ingredients
**Kaiserschmarrn**
- [ ] 4 eggs (size M)
- [ ] 150 g plain flour (Austrian *glatt*, type W480)
- [ ] 250 ml milk
- [ ] 30 g sugar for the batter + about 20 g (2 tbsp) to caramelise
- [ ] 1 pack vanilla sugar (about 8 g)
- [ ] 1 pinch salt
- [ ] 40 g raisins, soaked in 3 tbsp rum or water
- [ ] 40 g butter (20 g for the pan, 20 g at the end)
- [ ] Icing sugar for dusting

**Plum compote (*Zwetschkenröster*)**
- [ ] 500 g plums (*Zwetschken*)
- [ ] 100 g sugar
- [ ] 2 tbsp water
- [ ] 1 lemon slice
- [ ] 1 cinnamon stick
- [ ] 3 cloves

## Steps
1. [ ] **Raisins:** put **40 g raisins** with **3 tbsp rum** (or water) in a small bowl.
   ⏱ **30 min** — *at least; 1–2 hours is better.*
2. [ ] **Plum compote:** halve and stone **500 g plums**. Bring **100 g sugar**, **2 tbsp water**, **1 lemon slice**, **1 cinnamon stick** and **3 cloves** to the boil, add the plums and simmer on low heat. Let it cool a little, then remove the lemon and spices.
   ⏱ **10 min** — *plums soft but not falling apart.*
3. [ ] **Eischnee:** beat the **4 egg whites** with **1 pinch of salt** in a clean bowl until stiff. *Stop when they are stiff; don't over-beat.*
4. [ ] **Batter:** whisk the **4 yolks** with **30 g sugar** and **1 pack vanilla sugar** until frothy, then whisk in **250 ml milk** and **150 g flour** to a smooth, liquid batter.

   ![Whisking the yolk batter next to the eggshells](images/kaiserschmarrn/step-batter.jpg)
   *Whisk to a smooth liquid batter · Photo: [Servus](https://www.servus.com/r/altwiener-kaiserschmarrn)*

5. [ ] **Fold:** gently fold the Eischnee into the batter, only briefly, then stir in the **drained raisins**.

   ![Folding the Eischnee and raisins into the batter](images/kaiserschmarrn/step-fold.jpg)
   *Fold in the Eischnee, keep it airy · Photo: [Servus](https://www.servus.com/r/altwiener-kaiserschmarrn)*

6. [ ] **Bake:** melt **20 g butter** in a large pan over medium heat, pour in the batter (about **2 cm** high) and cook. *Lift the edge with a spatula to check that the underside is browned.*
   ⏱ **5 min** — *underside golden brown, top still soft.*

   ![Lifting the edge of the batter to check the browned underside](images/kaiserschmarrn/step-pan.jpg)
   *Check the underside · Photo: [Servus](https://www.servus.com/r/altwiener-kaiserschmarrn)*

7. [ ] **Flip:** cut the pancake into quarters with the spatula, turn the pieces over and bake until golden brown on the other side.
   ⏱ **4 min** — *golden brown all over.*
8. [ ] **Tear:** tear the pancake into pieces with **two forks**.

   ![Tearing the pancake into pieces with two forks](images/kaiserschmarrn/step-tear.jpg)
   *Rip it up with two forks · Photo: [Servus](https://www.servus.com/r/altwiener-kaiserschmarrn)*

9. [ ] **Caramelise:** sprinkle **about 20 g sugar** over the pieces, add the remaining **20 g butter** and toss the pieces a few times until the sugar caramelises slightly.

   ![Pieces with sugar and a knob of butter melting in the pan](images/kaiserschmarrn/step-butter.jpg)
   *Sugar and butter go in at the end · Photo: [Servus](https://www.servus.com/r/altwiener-kaiserschmarrn)*

   ![Tossing the pieces with a wooden spoon](images/kaiserschmarrn/step-toss.jpg)
   *Toss until lightly caramelised · Photo: [Servus](https://www.servus.com/r/altwiener-kaiserschmarrn)*

10. [ ] Dust with **icing sugar** and serve at once with the **warm plum compote**.

    ![Kaiserschmarrn in the pan with plum compote on the table](images/kaiserschmarrn/step-serve.jpg)
    *Serve with plum compote · Photo: [Servus](https://www.servus.com/r/kaiserschmarrn-mit-zwetschkenroester)*

## Timer cheat-sheet
| Step | Time | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| Raisins | 30 min+ | Plump |
| Plum compote | 10 min | Plums soft, not mushy |
| First side | 5 min | Golden underside |
| Second side | 4 min | Golden brown |

## If it goes wrong
- **Flat and dense:** the Eischnee was over-beaten or stirred in too hard. Fold gently and briefly.
- **Dry:** the batter was too thick (add milk) or it cooked too long.
- **Burnt outside, raw inside:** heat too high. Use moderate heat, or finish it in the oven.
- **Rubbery:** too much stirring after the flour went in.
- **Raisins burnt:** put them into the batter at the end, as above.

## Serve, store, reheat
Serve straight from the pan, dusted with icing sugar, with warm plum compote (or apple sauce). Kaiserschmarrn is best fresh; leftovers can be warmed briefly in a pan with a little butter. The compote keeps for several days in the fridge.

The flour varies from 25 to 44 g per egg; the median is about 37 g, which gives 150 g for 4 eggs. Milk is about 62 ml per egg in most recipes, so 250 ml. Fat and sugar vary a lot, so I took 40 g butter and about 50 g sugar in total, plus the compote.

## Variants
- **Oven finish (no flipping):** after about 5 minutes on the stove, finish under the oven grill on the lower rack (Servus).
- **With rum flambé:** briefly ignite rum in a ladle and pour it over the pieces (Servus).
- **With apple sauce** instead of plum compote.
