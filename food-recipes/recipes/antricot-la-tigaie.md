# Pan-Seared Ribeye (Antricot) with Butter Basting 🥩
*Antricot de vită la tigaie de fontă, cu basting în unt*

## The story
- **Origins:** *Antricot* comes from the French *entrecôte*, "between the ribs": meat from the rib region of the animal ([DEX](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/antricot)). The steak in the reel is a *Deluxe Antricot de vită Uruguay* from Lidl, on sale at 16.99 lei per 100 g (was 18.50) on the shelf tag in the cover photo.

  ![Shelf tag: Deluxe Antricot de vită Uruguay, on sale, 16.99 lei](images/antricot-la-tigaie/lidl.jpg)
  *The product in the reel, on the shelf · Photo: [Grill Mind reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRkLgnYiN5_/)*
- **How it evolved:** The reel shows the modern way to cook a thick steak: salt it **hours ahead** (a "dry brine"), cook it **by core temperature, not by the clock**, and finish with a **hot-butter bath** (*basting*). Gustos says you should salt either 40+ minutes before cooking or right before, never in the 5–15 minute window in between ([Gustos](https://www.gustos.ro/sfaturi-culinare/cum-sa-gatesti-perfect-un-antricot-de-vita-ghid-complet-pentru-steak-ca-la-restaurant.html)).
- **Fun fact:** The creator warns that a camera lies about meat colour: the same steak looks very different in daylight and kitchen light, so judge doneness with a thermometer, not with your eyes.

## Grill-master tips 🔥
*I searched for grandmother sources and found none for this dish, so these are the creator's and Romanian sites' tips.*
- **Pat the steak very dry after dry-brining.** The salt drew water to the surface; any moisture left blocks the browning reaction, so a dry surface gives a better crust. *The reel.*
- **Leave the garlic in its skin until the butter bath,** so it doesn't burn; then smash it hard to release the flavour. *The reel.*
- **Press the steak with a heavy weight** for even contact with the hot pan. *The reel.*
- **Cook by temperature, not time.** The creator says he is "against cooking by the clock", since you don't know your pan's real heat. *The reel.*
- **Keep the heat low for the butter,** or it burns and tastes bitter. *The reel.*
- **Pull the steak at 53–54 °C and eat at 55–56 °C:** the temperature climbs a little while it rests. *The reel.* Gustos puts medium-rare at about 55 °C and medium at 60 °C.
- **Add the butter only after the crust has formed,** not at the start. *Confirmed* ([Gustos](https://www.gustos.ro/sfaturi-culinare/cum-sa-gatesti-perfect-un-antricot-de-vita-ghid-complet-pentru-steak-ca-la-restaurant.html)).
- **Commenter's tip:** rest it under foil with the rosemary, a little pan butter and maybe garlic. *Tradition* (a comment by a viewer, not the creator).
- **Safety:** a cast-iron handle gets far above 100 °C, so use a cloth. *The reel.*

**Serves** 2 · **Prep** 20 min · **Dry-brine** 6 h · **Cook** about 20 min · **Medium**

> **Before you start:** salt the steak about 6 hours ahead and leave it uncovered in the fridge (step 1). You'll need a cast-iron skillet, a heavy press or weight, a probe thermometer and ideally an infrared thermometer.

## Mise en place
- [ ] Steak dry-brined and ready (step 1)
- [ ] 2–3 garlic cloves, **left in their skins**
- [ ] 1–2 rosemary sprigs (or thyme, or none)
- [ ] 30–50 g butter, cut into pieces *(typical)*
- [ ] A bunch of asparagus (about 200 g, trimmed) *(typical)*
- [ ] Paper towels, tongs, a big spoon, a plate and a cloth for the hot handle

## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 thick ribeye (*antricot*), about 450 g and at least 2–2.5 cm thick
- [ ] Coarse salt: **1% of the meat's weight**, about 5 g for 500 g *(typical)*
- [ ] 2–3 garlic cloves, unpeeled *(typical)*
- [ ] 1–2 rosemary sprigs
- [ ] 30–50 g butter *(typical)*
- [ ] About 200 g asparagus *(typical)*

## Steps
**Hours ahead (part one of the reel)**
1. [ ] **Dry-brine:** salt the steak all over with **about 5 g coarse salt (1% of its weight)** and leave it uncovered on a rack in the fridge. *The reel says about 6 hours; the salt amount comes from [csid.ro](https://www.csid.ro/diete/retete-culinare/cata-sare-trebuie-pusa-pe-carne-daca-faci-friptura-care-este-raportul-ideal-recomandat-de-chefi-21197098/).*
   ⏱ **6 h** — *the surface looks moist; that is the salt pulling water out.*

**Cooking**
2. [ ] Take the steak out and **pat it very dry** with paper towels, until no water is left on the surface.

   ![Patting the dry-brined steak with paper towel on a rack](images/antricot-la-tigaie/step-pat-dry.jpg)
   *Remove every drop of moisture · Photo: [Grill Mind reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRkLgnYiN5_/)*

3. [ ] Heat the **cast-iron skillet** until it is **very, very hot: at least 200 °C**. The creator uses the ceramic burner of a gas grill and checks the pan with an infrared thermometer.

   ![Infrared thermometer pointed at the empty skillet](images/antricot-la-tigaie/step-hot-pan.jpg)
   *Check the pan: 200 °C or more · Photo: [Grill Mind reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRkLgnYiN5_/)*

4. [ ] Start with the **fat side down**, holding the steak with tongs, until the fat renders and the pan is greased. *Go on when the fat has melted down and looks translucent.*

   ![Steak held fat-side down in the hot skillet](images/antricot-la-tigaie/step-fat.jpg)
   *Render the fat cap first · Photo: [Grill Mind reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRkLgnYiN5_/)*

5. [ ] Lay the steak flat in the pan and put the **heavy press** on top for even contact.

   ![Pressing the steak in the cast-iron skillet](images/antricot-la-tigaie/step-press.jpg)
   *The press flattens the steak onto the pan · Photo: [Grill Mind reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRkLgnYiN5_/)*

   ⏱ **2 min** — *the creator's "let's say 2 minutes", not a rule.*
6. [ ] **Flip.** The crust should already be evenly caramelized. Put the press back.

   ![Evenly browned crust on the first side](images/antricot-la-tigaie/step-crust.jpg)
   *Even, deep brown crust · Photo: [Grill Mind reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRkLgnYiN5_/)*

   ⏱ **2 min** — *mind the handle: it is far above 100 °C.*
7. [ ] **Flip again and take the core temperature** with the probe.

   ![Probe thermometer in the steak](images/antricot-la-tigaie/step-probe.jpg)
   *Cook by temperature, not by the clock · Photo: [Grill Mind reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRkLgnYiN5_/)*

8. [ ] Once the core is **past 32 °C (90 °F)**, turn the heat to **low**. Smash the unpeeled **2–3 garlic cloves** hard and add them with **30–50 g butter** and **1–2 rosemary sprigs**.

   ![Butter added to the pan next to the steak](images/antricot-la-tigaie/step-butter.jpg)
   *Butter in, heat down · Photo: [Grill Mind reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRkLgnYiN5_/)*

9. [ ] **Baste:** tilt the pan and keep spooning the hot, aromatic butter over the steak until the core reaches **53–54 °C (130 °F)**. *By temperature, not by time.* Take the steak out.

   ![Spooning hot butter over the seared steak](images/antricot-la-tigaie/step-baste.jpg)
   *The hot-butter bath · Photo: [Grill Mind reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRkLgnYiN5_/)*

10. [ ] **Rest** the steak on a plate. The creator calls this step "very important" but gives no time.
    ⏱ **7 min** — *5–7 minutes per [Gustos](https://www.gustos.ro/sfaturi-culinare/cum-sa-gatesti-perfect-un-antricot-de-vita-ghid-complet-pentru-steak-ca-la-restaurant.html), loosely covered with foil.* *(The reel gives no time.)*
11. [ ] Meanwhile cook the **asparagus** in the same fat as the steak, turning it often.
    ⏱ **3 min** — *bright green and lightly browned.* *(typical; the reel gives no time)*

    ![Asparagus cooking in the steak's pan](images/antricot-la-tigaie/step-asparagus.jpg)
    *Same pan, same fat · Photo: [Grill Mind reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRkLgnYiN5_/)*

12. [ ] **Slice** the steak across the grain into pieces about 1–1.5 cm thick ([Gustos](https://www.gustos.ro/sfaturi-culinare/cum-sa-gatesti-perfect-un-antricot-de-vita-ghid-complet-pentru-steak-ca-la-restaurant.html)) and serve with the asparagus. The result in the reel is about **medium**.

## Timer cheat-sheet
| Step | Time | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| Dry-brine | 6 h | Moist surface, uncovered in the fridge |
| Sear, 1st side | 2 min | Even caramelized crust |
| Sear, 2nd side | 2 min | Then probe the core |
| Baste | by temperature | Core 53–54 °C |
| Rest | 7 min | Temperature rises to 55–56 °C |
| Asparagus | 3 min | Bright green, lightly browned |

## If it goes wrong
- **Grey steak, weak crust:** the surface was wet or the pan not hot enough. Pat dry and heat to 200 °C or more.
- **Burnt, bitter butter:** the heat was too high. Add butter only after the crust forms, and lower the heat.
- **Overcooked:** pull it at 53–54 °C; it rises a few degrees while resting.
- **Too salty:** keep to about 1% of the meat's weight.
- **Tough steak:** rest it, and slice across the grain.

## Serve, store, reheat
Serve straight away with the asparagus and the pan juices. Leftover steak keeps 2 days in the fridge; eat it cold in slices or warm it very briefly so it doesn't overcook.

## Variants
- **Thyme instead of rosemary,** or no herb at all (the reel's own suggestion).
- **Cook to your doneness** with Gustos' temperatures: rare 50 °C, medium-rare 55 °C, medium 60 °C, medium-well 65 °C. Pull about 2 °C earlier.
