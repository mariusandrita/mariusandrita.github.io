# Romanian Eggplant Salad with Mayonnaise 🍆
*Salată de vinete cu maioneză*

![Eggplant salad on a plate, decorated with tomato and pepper](images/salata-de-vinete/finished.jpg)
*Photo: [Savori Urbane](https://savoriurbane.com/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-reteta-romaneasca-traditionala/)*

> **Source:** the base recipe and photos come from [Savori Urbane](https://savoriurbane.com/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-reteta-romaneasca-traditionala/) (5.0 from 9 reviews), cross-checked with four other Romanian sites. The yolks are mixed straight into the eggplant, so there is no separate mayonnaise to make.

## The story
- **Origins:** According to the Savori Urbane author, this salad has oriental (Turkish and Arab) roots and was adapted to Romanian tastes. Its cousins elsewhere use tahini and olive oil (baba ghanoush) or garlic and roasted pepper.
- **How it evolved:** The classic summer version is only eggplant, oil, onion and lemon or a splash of vinegar. The mayonnaise version came later and, as the author notes, it is "spornică" (it stretches): the yolks make a stable emulsion that takes up to about 50% more oil than the plain salad. Old Romanian kitchens chopped the eggplant with wooden tools, because plain steel knives turned it black.
- **Fun fact:** The author says that in her home the mayonnaise version is made only in winter, from baked and frozen eggplants, which are darker and wetter. In summer they eat the plain salad, which comes out white and creamy anyway.

## Grandma's tips 👵
- **Bake on an open flame** (gas ring or grill). The skin chars and the flesh stays white, fluffy and smoky; oven-baked or scalded eggplants lack that flavour. *Tradition* (the author, who learned it from her mother; [Savori Urbane](https://savoriurbane.com/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-reteta-romaneasca-traditionala/)).
- **Peel them while still hot** so the flesh stays as white as possible. *Confirmed* ([Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-locale/oradea/cum-sa-gatesti-cea-mai-gustoasa-salata-de-vinete-1724295.html), [Jamila Cuisine](https://jamilacuisine.ro/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-cremoasa-si-gustoasa-reteta-video/)).
- **Drain the flesh for 2–3 hours** in a colander; a lot of liquid comes out. *Confirmed* (Savori Urbane, [Laura Laurențiu](https://www.lauralaurentiu.ro/retete-culinare/retete-diverse/salata-de-vinete-romaneasca.html)).
- **Prick the skins with a fork before baking** so steam and liquid can escape. *Tradition* (Laura Laurențiu).
- **Chop with a long stainless knife or a wooden tool, not a food processor,** which kills the texture. *Tradition* (Laura Laurențiu).
- **Put the lemon and salt on the yolks first:** they help keep the salad's colour light. *The author's tip* (Savori Urbane).
- **Don't use olive oil:** it is too heavy and changes the flavour. Use sunflower oil. *The author's tip* (Savori Urbane).
- **If it tastes bitter,** mash the eggplant on a board with a wooden spoon, salt it and tilt the board to drain the bitter juice. *Tradition* (Adevărul).
- **If raw onion bothers you,** rinse the chopped onion briefly under cold water. *The author's tip* (Savori Urbane).

**Serves** 10 · **Prep** 60 min · **Bake** 30 min · **Drain** 2–3 h · **Chill** 1–2 h · **Medium**

> **Before you start:** the eggplants must drain for 2–3 hours, so bake them well ahead. Use a plastic colander and a hand mixer with paddles (not a blender).

## Mise en place
- [ ] Wash 1.5–1.7 kg eggplants (dark, shiny, plump)
- [ ] Finely chop 1 small onion (or crush 2–3 garlic cloves)
- [ ] Cut the lemon
- [ ] Measure **150 ml** sunflower oil in a jug; you'll use it in small portions
- [ ] Separate **3 fresh egg yolks**

## Ingredients
- [ ] 1.5–1.7 kg fresh eggplants (about 850 g of flesh after baking and draining)
- [ ] 3 fresh egg yolks (1 yolk per 300 g of baked, drained eggplant)
- [ ] 150–200 ml sunflower oil
- [ ] Juice of about ½ lemon (about 2 tsp to start, then to taste)
- [ ] Salt (about 2 good pinches to start, then to taste)
- [ ] 1 small onion, finely chopped, **or** 2–3 crushed garlic cloves
- [ ] To serve: tomatoes, peppers or chopped spring onion, and bread

## Steps
1. [ ] **Bake** **1.5–1.7 kg eggplants** directly over an open flame (gas ring or grill), turning them, until the skin is charred and the flesh is very soft. *Prick the skins with a fork first.*
   ⏱ **30 min** — *skin black and blistered, flesh collapsing.*
2. [ ] **Peel them while hot** and scoop the flesh into a plastic colander.
3. [ ] **Drain** the flesh. *Weigh it afterwards: you want about **850 g**.*
   ⏱ **3 h** — *drain 2–3 hours; a lot of liquid comes out.*

   ![Baked, peeled eggplant draining in a colander](images/salata-de-vinete/step-drain.jpg)
   *Draining the baked eggplant · Photo: [Savori Urbane](https://savoriurbane.com/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-reteta-romaneasca-traditionala/)*

4. [ ] **Chop** the drained flesh finely on a big board with a long stainless knife. *Not a food processor.*

   ![Finely chopped baked eggplant on a board](images/salata-de-vinete/step-chop.jpg)
   *Chop finely with a knife · Photo: [Savori Urbane](https://savoriurbane.com/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-reteta-romaneasca-traditionala/)*

5. [ ] Chop **1 small onion** very finely (or crush **2–3 garlic cloves**) and cut the **lemon**. *If raw onion bothers you, rinse it briefly under cold water.*

   ![Finely chopped onion and a halved lemon](images/salata-de-vinete/step-mise.jpg)
   *Onion and lemon ready · Photo: [Savori Urbane](https://savoriurbane.com/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-reteta-romaneasca-traditionala/)*

6. [ ] Put the **850 g chopped eggplant** in a roomy bowl. Add **3 egg yolks**, then squeeze **about 2 tsp lemon juice** and add **2 good pinches of salt** right onto the yolks.

   ![Three yolks on top of the chopped eggplant in a bowl](images/salata-de-vinete/step-yolks.jpg)
   *Yolks, lemon and salt go in first · Photo: [Savori Urbane](https://savoriurbane.com/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-reteta-romaneasca-traditionala/)*

7. [ ] Mix with a **hand mixer with paddles or whisk (not a blender)** while adding the **150 ml sunflower oil** gradually, in small portions. *The mayonnaise forms together with the salad. Don't add all the oil at once; this salad soaks it up greedily, so keep to the 150 ml you measured (up to 200 ml at most).*
   ⏱ **2 min** — *1–2 minutes, no more; the mixture turns pale and fluffy.*

   ![The eggplant mixture, pale and creamy after mixing with the oil](images/salata-de-vinete/step-mix.jpg)
   *Pale and fluffy · Photo: [Savori Urbane](https://savoriurbane.com/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-reteta-romaneasca-traditionala/)*

8. [ ] Stir in the **chopped onion** (or **crushed garlic**) with a spoon. Taste, then adjust the **salt and lemon**.

   ![The finished creamy, white eggplant salad](images/salata-de-vinete/step-creamy.jpg)
   *Creamy, white and aromatic · Photo: [Savori Urbane](https://savoriurbane.com/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-reteta-romaneasca-traditionala/)*

9. [ ] Move to a covered container and **chill**. It turns creamier and firmer. Serve decorated with tomatoes, peppers or chopped spring onion, with bread.
   ⏱ **2 h** — *1 hour at least ([Gustos](https://www.gustos.ro/retete-culinare/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-13.html)); 1–2 hours is better.*

   ![Slice of bread spread with eggplant salad and diced tomato](images/salata-de-vinete/step-serve.jpg)
   *Serve on fresh bread with summer tomatoes · Photo: [Savori Urbane](https://savoriurbane.com/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-reteta-romaneasca-traditionala/)*

## Timer cheat-sheet
| Step | Time | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| Bake over flame | 30 min | Skin charred, flesh collapsing |
| Drain | 2–3 h | About 850 g of flesh left |
| Mix with oil | 1–2 min | Pale, fluffy, creamy |
| Chill | 1–2 h | Creamier and firmer |

## If it goes wrong
- **Greasy or oily:** too much oil. Measure 150 ml first; the salad absorbs it greedily.
- **Watery:** drain longer (2–3 hours) and chop only after draining.
- **Dark or grey salad:** peel the eggplants while hot, avoid plain carbon-steel knives, and add lemon and salt early.
- **Bitter:** mash on a board with a wooden spoon, salt, and tilt to drain the juice (Adevărul).
- **Not creamy:** use mixer paddles (not a blender) and add the oil in small portions.
- **Tastes flat:** salt and lemon, a little at a time.

## Serve, store, reheat
Serve cold, decorated with tomatoes and peppers, on fresh bread. Keep it covered in the fridge; it is good the next day, straight from the fridge. It contains **raw egg yolks**: use very fresh eggs, keep it cold, and eat it within 1–2 days *(typical advice; the author notes that raw yolks are perishable and that this version suits winter better than hot summer days)*.

## Grocery list
- [ ] **Produce:** eggplants 1.5–1.7 kg, 1 small onion (or garlic), 1 lemon, tomatoes and peppers or spring onion to serve
- [ ] **Dairy and eggs:** 3 fresh eggs (for the yolks)
- [ ] **Pantry:** sunflower oil (200 ml), salt, bread

## Equipment
Gas ring or grill for the eggplants, plastic colander, long stainless knife, big board, roomy bowl, hand mixer with paddles or whisk.

## Sources compared
Base: [Savori Urbane](https://savoriurbane.com/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-reteta-romaneasca-traditionala/) (850 g flesh, 3 yolks, 150–200 ml oil, drain 2–3 h). Cross-checks: [Jamila Cuisine](https://jamilacuisine.ro/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-cremoasa-si-gustoasa-reteta-video/) (separate mayonnaise, oven 200 °C for 40–50 min, chill for hours), [Laura Laurențiu](https://www.lauralaurentiu.ro/retete-culinare/retete-diverse/salata-de-vinete-romaneasca.html) (prick skins, avoid food processor, yolk-and-mustard variant), [Gustos](https://www.gustos.ro/retete-culinare/salata-de-vinete-cu-maioneza-13.html) (1.5 kg eggplants gives 750 g, 200 ml oil, chill 1 h) and [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-locale/oradea/cum-sa-gatesti-cea-mai-gustoasa-salata-de-vinete-1724295.html) (peel hot, fix for bitterness). The others make a **separate mayonnaise** with a whole egg or yolk and mustard; this recipe skips the mustard and mixes the yolks straight in. The oil amounts differ (about 60 ml for 2 eggplants up to 200 ml for 750–850 g), so I follow the base recipe's 150 ml.

## Variants
- **Simple summer version (without mayonnaise):** oil, chopped onion and lemon or a splash of vinegar only.
- **With separate mayonnaise:** make it from yolk, mustard, oil and lemon, then fold it in (Jamila, Gustos).
- **Garlic instead of onion:** 2–3 crushed cloves.
