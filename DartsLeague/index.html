Capabilities

What's inside

🔴
Real-time WebSocket sync
Socket.IO keeps every dart synced between controller and TV the moment it's entered.
🎯
X01 — 301, 501, 701
Optional double-out, up to 10 players, automatic leg rotation, configurable throw order.
🎊
Celebration overlays
180s, bull finishes, funny awards — animated overlays fire on the TV for big moments.
🔊
Romanian score reader
The controller reads scores aloud in Romanian using the browser's speech synthesis API.
📊
Stats & player spotlight
3-dart average, legs won, 180s, high finish — rotating lobby display between matches.
🗄️
Local-first SQLite
All data on your hardware. Runs on a Raspberry Pi 4B. No cloud, no accounts, no internet.

Built with
