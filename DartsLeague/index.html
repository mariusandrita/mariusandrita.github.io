Setup in minutes

How it works

1
We set it up
We install and configure DartsLeague on a small device — an old laptop or Raspberry Pi works fine. Connect it to your pub's Wi-Fi.
2
Plug into the TV
HDMI from the device to your screen. Open a browser on any phone and you're ready to run a match.
3
Play
Create a match, pick your players, start throwing. The TV updates live. Stats save automatically after every game.
